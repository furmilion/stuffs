Best viewed with a monospace font.

These modules were created for the purpose of being played in my custom Infinitode 2 map.
The LQ/INFINITODE 2 module is the one heard in the game, and most samples were shrinked down to audible artifacts to fit under ~400kb limitation
due to the fact that the game uses Base64 encoding in the music block. I, however, think that those artifacts
give off a kind of "signature" sound.

The HQ module is the LQ module but with samples pulled from the source, retaining as-is quality and thus sounding way cleaner.
